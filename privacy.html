Salah Time — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Salah Time is an offline prayer-times app for Android. It has no user accounts, advertising or analytics, and does not send your data to us or to a service we operate or control.

What the app uses

  • Approximate / precise location (optional). Used on the device to calculate prayer times for your area. Coordinates are stored only in the app's private storage. If a fresh fix is unavailable, the app uses the last resolved location or its built-in default city.
  • Reverse geocoding. To display a city name, the app uses the geocoding service built into your device. Depending on the device, that system service may send coordinates to the platform vendor's geocoding provider. The app itself has no server and does not upload coordinates.
  • Notifications. If enabled, local prayer-time notifications are generated and shown on the device.
  • Local storage. Settings, calculated schedules and home-screen widget data are stored in app-private SharedPreferences / Hive storage. Uninstalling the app removes them.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or personal identifiers.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of data with third parties.

Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionPurpose
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATIONCalculate prayer times for your position. Location is optional; a saved or default city is used if unavailable.
POST_NOTIFICATIONSShow prayer-time notifications you enable.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_EXACT_ALARMStart prayer notifications at their scheduled minute. If unavailable, the app continues with an inexact one-minute window.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDRe-arm notifications after a device restart.
INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEAvailable to the platform location and geocoding services. Prayer times are calculated locally.

Children

The app collects no personal data and is suitable for all audiences.
